# Limitations and ethics

**Version:** 0.2.0-draft

## Limitations

1. **Not production-ready.** Educational strategy prototype only.  
2. **Synthetic data.** Archetypes and scenarios are constructed; they are not sampled from confidential portfolios.  
3. **Illustrative thresholds.** Policy cutoffs teach structure; they are not optimized.  
4. **No predictive accuracy claim.** Do not cite outputs as forecasts of real merchant default or loss.  
5. **Incomplete legal coverage.** Taxonomy is not a jurisdiction-complete compliance program.  
6. **Content signals are aggregated proxies.** No claim of automated moderation quality.  
7. **Missing internal features.** Production systems use richer settlement, network, device, and case-management data.  
8. **Fairness not evaluated.** No protected-class features; no disparate-impact analysis performed.  

## Ethics and data governance

- Do not upload real personal, customer, or confidential merchant data into the demo.  
- Prefer aggregated features; practice data minimization.  
- Disclose synthetic status on data files, API responses, and UI.  
- Separate hard policy from scoring; keep reasons human-readable.  
- Any future production path requires legal, privacy, fairness, and model-risk governance.

## Separation of claims

| Claim class | Handling |
|-------------|----------|
| External research | Cited with as-of dates in research report |
| Strategic inference | Labeled as inference |
| Demo assumptions | Documented in synthetic methodology and policy |
| Needs internal validation | Called out explicitly; not asserted as proven |
