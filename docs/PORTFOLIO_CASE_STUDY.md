# Portfolio case study

**Project:** Digital Goods Merchant Risk Strategy Lab  
**Owner:** Charlene Yang  
**Status:** Phase 2 documentation foundation in progress

## Executive summary

| | |
|--|--|
| **Problem** | Digital-goods merchants challenge payment underwriting built for physical commerce. |
| **Strategic question** | How should platforms evaluate and control merchants with intangible, immediate, high-frequency fulfillment and thin traditional evidence? |
| **Approach** | Research + taxonomy + content-risk framing + synthetic scenarios + explainable rules/score demonstrator. |
| **Deliverable** | Docs, API, and interactive scenario lab for portfolio use. |
| **Key insight** | Risk is interaction-driven (ticket × frequency × prepaid × growth × content integrity)—not a single rate. |
| **Limitations** | Synthetic/illustrative; not production; no confidential data; no predictive accuracy claim. |

## Narrative (2-minute path)

1. **Market shift** — Mobile IAP/subscriptions at large scale; digital monetization expands beyond physical shipping models ([RESEARCH_REPORT.md](RESEARCH_REPORT.md)).  
2. **Framework adaptation** — Shipping evidence and recoverable inventory assumptions weaken.  
3. **Category differences** — Short drama, web fiction, games, and AI services stress different controls.  
4. **Risk taxonomy** — Payment/fraud, credit/exposure, compliance/integrity, operations/reputation.  
5. **Content monitoring** — Content failure pathways into refunds, chargebacks, removal, and credit exposure.  
6. **Decision and reserve framework** — Explainable actions and illustrative reserves.  
7. **Interactive scenarios** — Archetypes and stress comparisons (Phase 5).  
8. **Example findings** — Pending engine demos (Phase 3–5).  
9. **Best practices** — Risk-based onboarding, progressive limits, exposure-based controls, integrity monitoring, governance.  
10. **Limitations and next steps** — Internal data validation, legal/model-risk review if ever productionized.

## Personal contribution

Product framing, research synthesis, risk taxonomy design, policy design, architecture, implementation, and portfolio packaging.
