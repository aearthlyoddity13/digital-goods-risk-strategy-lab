# Interview talk track

**Duration options:** 90 seconds · 5 minutes · deep dive

## 90 seconds

“I built a Digital Goods Merchant Risk Strategy Lab for payment platforms. Digital merchants—short drama, web fiction, games, AI services—don’t fit physical-goods underwriting: fulfillment is intangible, consumption can be instant, and prepaid value creates contingent exposure. I combined industry research, a risk taxonomy, a content-risk-to-payments framework, and an explainable rules-and-score demonstrator on synthetic merchant scenarios. The point isn’t a black-box score—it’s showing when to approve, monitor, delay settlement, reserve, review, or decline, and why content integrity can become financial exposure. Everything is synthetic and explicitly not a production model.”

## 5 minutes

1. Problem and central question  
2. Digital vs physical comparison table (one example: representment evidence)  
3. Taxonomy walkthrough (pick one risk per family)  
4. Content → payments pathways  
5. Demo: healthy growth vs low-ticket fraud emergence vs content deterioration  
6. Limitations and what you’d need internally to validate  

## Likely questions

| Question | Answer direction |
|----------|------------------|
| Is this what PayPal uses? | No. Portfolio research demonstrator; no confidential data or policies. |
| Why not ML? | Strategy and explainability first; ML needs labeled internal outcomes and governance. |
| How did you set thresholds? | Illustrative, version-controlled; teach interactions, not claim optimality. |
| How does content enter credit? | Interruption and dispute pathways; aggregated indicators only. |
| What’s your contribution? | Problem framing, taxonomy, policy design, system design, implementation, docs. |

## Phrases to avoid

“AI-powered,” “production-ready,” “industry-leading,” “highly accurate,” “trained on real processor data.”
